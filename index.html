Emma Holly Jones

Director

Emma Holly Jones is a British director and screenwriter based in Los Angeles. Her debut feature, Mr. Malcolm’s List, was released worldwide by Universal and Bleecker Street, earning two Irish Film & TV Academy nominations, an NAACP Image Award nomination and a World Soundtrack Award for its score. She began her career in photography and documentary.
